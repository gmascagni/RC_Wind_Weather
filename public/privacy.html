Privacy Policy — RC Wind Weather Pro

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 • Version: v3.2.0

Application Package ID: com.rcwind.weatherpro

1. Overview & Zero-Tracking Commitment

RC Wind Weather Pro is designed specifically for radio-controlled (RC) aircraft pilots, aeromodelers, and flight clubs to evaluate runway crosswinds, peak gusts, density altitude, and flight safety envelopes. Your privacy is paramount: we do not collect, store on external servers, monetize, sell, or share any personal or identifiable user data. No user accounts or sign-ins are required.

2. Data Collection, Permissions & Usage

A. Precise & Coarse Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

When you tap the "GPS" button, the application requests foreground location access solely to determine your current flying field coordinates (latitude and longitude). These coordinates are sent directly over encrypted HTTPS to the public Open-Meteo Weather API and OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse-geocoding service to retrieve local wind speed, wind gusts, wind direction, temperature, and field elevation.

  • Location access is strictly ephemeral and foreground-only (triggered only when you request it).
  • The app never tracks your location in the background.
  • Your coordinates are never sent to the developer or linked to any personal identifier.

B. Device Sensors (Magnetometer, Accelerometer & Orientation)

The "Live Compass" and "Phone Aim Wind Calibration" features read your device's compass heading in real time to align the runway strip and wind vector with your physical orientation at the flying field. Sensor readings are processed 100% locally in device memory and are never recorded or transmitted off your device.

C. Local Device Storage (localStorage)

User preferences—including saved favorite flying fields, runway headings, wind speed units (MPH, KT, KM/H), pilot skill profiles (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Extreme), calculation basis (Gust vs. Sustained), and audience humor mode (Adult vs. Kid-Friendly)—are stored exclusively on your device using local browser/WebView storage.

3. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

Because RC Wind Weather Pro does not maintain user accounts or external databases, no personal data is retained by the developer. You have full control over all locally stored preferences and cached weather data on your device:

  • In-App Removal: You can delete any saved favorite location at any time by tapping the × button next to the favorite, or reset your limits inside the LIMITS dialog.
  • Full Reset: Clearing the application's storage/cache in your Android Device Settings or uninstalling the application permanently erases all locally saved preferences.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Weather forecasts and city/ZIP geocoding lookups are fulfilled via the open-source Open-Meteo API over HTTPS. RC Wind Weather Pro contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero behavioral analytics trackers, and zero marketing cookies.

5. Audience Profiles & Children’s Privacy

RC Wind Weather Pro includes both a comedic "Adult (18+)" flight-line mode and a 100% family-friendly "Kid-Friendly / Cadet" mode with clean educational aviation terminology. The application does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults under COPPA, GDPR, or CCPA.

6. Contact & Support

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, app permissions, or support, please contact the developer via the official RC Wind Weather Pro developer support email listed on our Google Play Store page.
