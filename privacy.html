LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy — RC Wind Weather Pro

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Application: RC Wind Weather Pro (com.rcwindweather.pro)

1. Overview

RC Wind Weather Pro is designed specifically for radio-controlled (RC) aircraft pilots, aeromoders, and flight clubs to evaluate runway crosswinds, peak gusts, and flight safety envelopes. Your privacy is paramount: we do not collect, monetize, sell, or track your personal information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

A. GPS & Location Data

If you choose to use the "Locate by GPS" feature, the app accesses your device's geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude) solely to identify local weather telemetry and magnetic runway alignment. This data is transmitted directly via encrypted HTTPS to the Open-Meteo weather API to retrieve wind and meteorological conditions. Location coordinates are never transmitted to our own servers and are never linked to your personal identity.

B. Device Sensors (Magnetometer & Compass)

The "Live Compass" feature reads your device's heading to align the flight runway strip and wind indicator with your physical heading at the field. Sensor data is processed entirely in real-time on your device and is never recorded or transmitted.

C. Local Preferences Storage

Settings such as saved flying field favorites, runway headings, wind unit preferences (mph, kt, km/h), pilot experience levels, and content profiles are stored strictly on your local device storage. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your application cache or uninstalling the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Weather forecasts and geocoding city search queries are served through the public Open-Meteo weather API under standard encrypted HTTPS requests. No third-party advertising SDKs, tracking cookies, or analytics services are integrated into RC Wind Weather Pro.

4. Children’s Privacy

RC Wind Weather Pro offers a dedicated "Kid-Friendly / Cadet" profile specifically to provide safe, clean, educational flight-safety terminology for young pilots and club cadets. The app does not collect personal information from children or any users.
